Questions owners ask.

Straight answers in plain words. Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp.

I'm not technical at all. Is that a problem?
Not at all. Tell us about your business on WhatsApp, in Hindi or English, by text or voice note. We handle all the technical work and explain everything in simple words.
How much does a website cost?
It depends on how many pages and features you need. Message us on WhatsApp with what you have in mind and we'll send a clear price before any work begins.
How long does it take?
It depends on the size of the site. We share the timeline together with the price, so you know your launch date before we start.
Do I need to buy a domain and hosting?
If you already have them, great. If not, we help you choose a domain name and get everything set up.
Will my website show up on Google?
We set up the basics that help Google understand your site: page titles, descriptions, a sitemap and your location. Ranking higher takes time, and we'll tell you honestly what helps.
Do you work with businesses outside India?
Yes. We work over WhatsApp and email, so your location doesn't matter.
